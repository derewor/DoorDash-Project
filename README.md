# DoorDash-Project
This project examines in detail the cancellation of orders.

# Introduction
In this project the cancelled orders were analyzed in order to find out:
1. Who is responsible for most of the cancellations i.e the system or the clients themselves?
2. Does the cancellation depends on the assignment of drivers for the delivery?
3. Does the cancellation show variability across the times of the day?
4. How quickly the orders are cancelled and how do they vary based on driver assignment?
# Key Findings
* The cancellation by clients at least 2-fold higher than the cancellation by the system itself.
* The large majority of cancellation (>60%) are caused by failure to assign a driver.
* The cancellation of orders is at its highest around 8 AM in the morning and it is also considerably high at night between 8 PM and 3AM.
* Clients cancel the order within 2 min a driver is not assigned. If a driver is assigned the wait a maximum of 4 minutes before canceling the order.

# Assessement
* The fact that there are more cancellation in at 8 AM and at night suggests that there are opportunities to improve business profitability through engaging more drivers during these periods.
